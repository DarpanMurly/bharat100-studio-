About

Why we're building this.

Bharat@100 - Vision 2047 exists because India's growth story is usually told in two disconnected registers - dense policy language, or vague optimism. Neither one is something you can actually check, learn from, or carry into your own work.

So every post here is small, sourced and specific: a real number, a real date, a real decision someone made. Over the next twenty years, on the way to India's 100th year of independence, that adds up to something worth watching.

This is one person's project, built and run independently. It moves quickly precisely because it isn't trying to be an institution.

A note on independence

Bharat@100 - Vision 2047 is an independent citizen project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of the Government of India or any of its agencies.

Every fact carries its sources. When a claim can't be verified against at least one independent report, it doesn't get posted.
