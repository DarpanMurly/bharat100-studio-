Fourteen stories this week, leaning hard into history - a wartime last stand, a space launch, a parliamentary renaming, and the birth of ISRO - alongside the usual mix of sector records and builder stories that keep showing up no matter how crowded the news cycle gets. Here's the week, day by day.

## Tuesday, November 17

On the 98th anniversary of his death, the story of Lala Lajpat Rai - "Punjab Kesari" - whose death after a police lathi-charge pushed a new generation of revolutionaries, including Bhagat Singh, toward independence.

## Wednesday, November 18

On the 64th anniversary of the Battle of Rezang La, the story of Charlie Company's last stand in Ladakh - 120 men who held a mountain pass until they had nothing left to fight with, and the Param Vir Chakra it earned their commander.

## Thursday, November 19

On the anniversary of her first launch, the story of Kalpana Chawla's path from Karnal, Haryana to the flight deck of Space Shuttle Columbia - a small-town engineering degree that carried her all the way to orbit.

## Friday, November 20

PV Sindhu's first Super Series Premier title, ten years back, and a fresh government assessment of India's PLI schemes that's honest about where the money landed (76% of its investment target) and where the jobs didn't (just 28%).

## Saturday, November 21

The birth of India's space program at Thumba - rocket parts moved by bicycle and bullock cart in 1963 - alongside a record month for Coal India's coal supply, and the ex-ISRO engineer who now points satellites at crop fields instead of orbit.

## Sunday, November 22

Parliament's 1968 vote renaming Madras State to Tamil Nadu, an Indian-raised economist's new role as Chief Economist at the Asian Development Bank, a record month for foreign investment in Indian stocks, and a founder giving away 100,000 pairs of glasses a year after taking his company public.

## Monday, November 23

Jagadish Chandra Bose's uncredited radio breakthrough - work that may have predated Marconi's, never patented - a national milestone of 3.76 crore rural women crossing a lakh rupees in household income, and the entrepreneur whose solar dehydrators are stopping harvests from going to waste before they reach a market.

Fourteen stories, one week. A mountain pass held to the last round, a flight deck reached from a small Haryana town, a renaming voted into law, and a number - 3.76 crore - that only exists because millions of smaller numbers, each one a single household, added up to it.
